The Great MScape

by Liam

How to Play

Press W, SPACE, or UP to jump

Don't fly into the top of the screen. A plane will fly in and hit you

Press A or LEFT to go back, D or RIGHT to go forward

After 3 left or 3 right presses, those keys rest for a bit

Don't let a Skittle eat you

If the giant Skittle catches you, it jumps up. You teleport under it and try to escape. Then it smashes you

Hungry Skittles come from the left, one at a time, and chase you until they eat you, a bird, or another Skittle

Don't touch the birds

The giant Skittle eats birds and other Skittles

Score increases with each successful jump

Finish a level to start the next one. There's a checkpoint at the end of level 1 and level 2

If you die, press Checkpoint to start at your last checkpoint

Don't bump the right wall more than 10 times

Press P to pause

Consecutive jumps give bonus points!
