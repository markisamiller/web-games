    How to Play

    Play Snack Squad, fight a friend on one computer, or play online.

    Snack Squad: you are Coke. Pepsi, Hershey, and Mars are computer fighters. They team up and each punch does 10 damage. A D move, W jump, Space punch.

    Online: everyone uses A D to move, W to jump, Space to punch.

    Same computer:

    Coke: A and D to move, W to jump, Space punch

    Pepsi: arrow keys to move, up to jump, K to punch

    Hershey: J and L to move, I to jump, U to punch

    Mars: 1 and 3 to move, 2 to jump, 4 to punch

    Punch the other foods. Last snack standing wins.

    Walk over the sparkles (tiny glowing bits) to heal 25 health. Or press the Heal button.

    Press P to pause.

    Paused
