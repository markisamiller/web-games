Home World

A city that never ends. Walk, jump, and grab stars.

You start in watch mode. WASD fly. Space up. Shift down.

Press 1 to play in first person. Press 2 to play behind you. Press 3 to watch again.

A walks left. D walks right. W walks forward. S walks back. Hold Shift to sprint when you play.

Hold the right mouse button and move to look around.

Some buildings have brown ladders. Walk into one and press Space or W to climb onto the roof.

Walk into a glowing door to play another game.
